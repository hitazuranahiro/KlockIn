# KlockIN offline map data

## Philippine city and municipal boundaries

`philippines-municipalities.geojson` comes from the Philippine municipal boundary dataset published by geoBoundaries gbOpen. It represents 2020 administrative boundaries compiled by the National Mapping and Resource Information Authority (NAMRIA), the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), and OCHA Philippines. The original boundaries are available under CC BY 3.0 IGO, and geoBoundaries publishes its standardized distribution under CC BY 4.0. [GeoBoundaries dataset metadata](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/PHL/ADM3/), [OCHA/HDX source](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/philippines-administrative-levels-0-to-3), [CC BY 3.0 IGO](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/), [geoBoundaries attribution and terms](https://www.geoboundaries.org/), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

KlockIN adapted the pinned ADM3 geometry from `9469f09`. It combined the 14 source Manila district features into one City of Manila feature. The separate ADM2 City of Manila boundary from `41af8f1` was used to identify the relevant district features and verify their combined coverage. The PSA identifies City of Manila as a single city at the requested administrative level. [Pinned ADM3 GeoJSON](https://media.githubusercontent.com/media/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries/9469f09/releaseData/gbOpen/PHL/ADM3/geoBoundaries-PHL-ADM3_simplified.geojson), [ADM2 metadata](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/PHL/ADM2/), [PSA listing](https://psa.gov.ph/classification/psgc/cities/1300000000).

KlockIN simplified the shared boundary geometry with Mapshaper 0.8.10 at 58% weighted retention with `keep-shapes` and 0.0001 degree coordinate precision. The resulting file contains 1,634 valid polygons or multipolygons in WGS 84 longitude/latitude order, with the source feature identifier and display name. The geometry provides approximate locality labels. Boundaries and names may differ near municipal edges or where jurisdictions have changed since 2020. [Mapshaper simplification guide](https://mapshaper.org/docs/guides/simplification.html), [GeoJSON specification](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc7946).

## Philippine major roads

`philippines-major-roads.geojson` comes from Natural Earth 1:10m Roads version 5.0.0. KlockIN retained `Major Highway` road features, clipped them to the sourced Philippine land boundaries, simplified the geometry for display, and kept available source names and route references. The 823 road features show an overview of selected highways at the original cartographic scale. Natural Earth distributes these vectors in the public domain. [Natural Earth roads](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/roads/), [Archive](https://naturalearth.s3.amazonaws.com/10m_cultural/ne_10m_roads.zip), [Natural Earth terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).
